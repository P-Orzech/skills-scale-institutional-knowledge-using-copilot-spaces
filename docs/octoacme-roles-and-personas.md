# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
Designs user interfaces that align with product requirements and usability standards.

### Responsibilities
- Conducts user research and creates mockups/prototypes.
- Collaborates with product and development teams to refine solutions.
- Works closely with Product Managers and Developers to ensure a seamless user experience and clarity around acceptance criteria.

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce friction and improve task completion rates
- Ensure designs meet accessibility and usability standards

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and prototype walkthroughs
- Usability test reports and research findings
- Collaboration via design tools, spec docs, and sprint reviews

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Documents product features, API, and processes for both user and internal consumption.

### Responsibilities
- Translates technical details into accessible language.
- Maintains change logs and reviews accuracy of key artifacts.
- Engages with Developers, QA, and Product Managers to ensure documentation completeness.

### Goals
- Ensure all features and APIs are clearly documented
- Reduce support burden through comprehensive user guides
- Keep documentation accurate and up-to-date with each release

### Typical Communication
- Doc review sessions and feedback cycles
- Change logs and release notes
- Collaboration via wikis, issue trackers, and pull request reviews

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Plans, coordinates, and communicates deployment schedules.

### Responsibilities
- Ensures all release criteria are met and manages versioning.
- Collaborates on incident response or rollback if needed.
- Interfaces with Project Management, QA, and Developers throughout the release lifecycle.

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, on-schedule releases with minimal disruption
- Maintain clear versioning and release history
- Enable rapid rollback in case of incidents

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and go/no-go meetings
- Deployment schedules and release notes
- Incident communication during and after deployments

---

## Customer Support

### Role Summary
Acts as the voice of the user, reporting issues and providing feedback on delivered features.

### Responsibilities
- Escalates urgent incidents and triages bug reports.
- Relays common questions to the Product and Development teams.
- Supports user onboarding and issue resolution post-release.

### Goals
- Resolve user issues quickly and accurately
- Surface actionable feedback to product and engineering teams
- Improve user satisfaction and retention

### Typical Communication
- Support tickets and incident escalations
- Bug reports and feature feedback to development teams
- User onboarding sessions and post-release check-ins

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Gathers business requirements, maps processes, and defines acceptance metrics for success.

### Responsibilities
- Translates stakeholder needs into actionable work items.
- Clarifies assumptions and participates in backlog refinement.
- Works with Product Managers, Developers, and Stakeholders to ensure alignment with business goals.

### Goals
- Ensure delivered solutions align with business objectives
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and acceptance criteria
- Improve efficiency of backlog refinement and planning

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Process maps, user stories, and acceptance criteria docs
- Backlog refinement and planning sessions

---
